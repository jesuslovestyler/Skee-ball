Flick the ball up the lane — a quick snap throws harder than a slow drag.

Throw: press on the ball, flick upward and let go. The flick's speed is the power; its sideways slant is the aim. Keyboard: hold Space to charge the power meter, tap ← / → to aim, release Space to roll. Too soft and the ball rolls back for 0; a near-perfect full-power throw angled just right can drop a corner 100.
